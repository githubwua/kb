# Head/Skull/neck/Upper spine/chest 虚領頂勁 含胸抜背

tuck your chin in by tucking your nose and pushing your forehead forward. the focus in not on your chin, by rather your nose and forehead. this helps pull your neck back and align your skull directly above the spine.
at the same time, focus on the highest backbone where the name tag of your t-shirt touches. the back bone where your spine meets the neck.  pull this bone back and up
at the same time, pull the mid spine back and down.  This is the bone in your spine behind the base of the chest bone, i.e behing the mizuochi, at about the height of your elbows.
at the same time, pull collar bones back and away the neck, your shoulders should indirectly be pulled back and away from the neck as a result.

practice this will a towel over your head, and pulling both ends down with your hands, while your head pushing up to resist the pull.


# tips

Don't use force. Use your mind or intention to move your bones, not your muscles.
instead of pulling shoulders back, pull your collar bones back.

if you find yourself pulling back too much and starting to lose balance, push your chest bone and groin forward to rebalance
